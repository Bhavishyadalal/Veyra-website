GOOD TO KNOW / WEBSITE PRIVACY

A quieter corner
of the internet.

This notice describes this Veyra showcase website. It does not describe the Android app or third-party music providers.

No sign-up. No tracking scripts.

The site has no account system, newsletter form, advertising scripts, embedded social widgets or analytics code. Fonts, illustrations and the Veyra icon are served with the website rather than requested from a font or image CDN.

Pointer and sharing controls.

The decorative desktop cursor responds to pointer movement locally. Pointer coordinates are not stored or sent to a server. Touch devices retain their usual behavior. The copy-link button writes the public website address to your clipboard only when you press it; if access is unavailable, the website offers a selectable link instead.

A couple of local preferences.

Your motion preference is saved in your browser’s local storage as veyra-site-motion. A session-storage flag, veyra-v2-intro, keeps the short opening reveal from repeating within the same tab session. These values are not sent by the website to an analytics service. You can remove them by clearing this site’s browser data.

The listening preview.

The preview uses illustrative track names and artwork. Its play button animates a visual progress bar; it does not stream music, access a microphone or play sound. Preview likes stay in the current page’s memory and disappear when you reload.

Hosting and external links.

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP addresses, browser headers and server logs under its own policy. If the site is deployed on Vercel, refer to Vercel’s privacy policy. GitHub links and source downloads take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

App privacy is separate.

Online app features connect to their respective providers and servers. Review the application source and the policies of any services you use. This website makes no claim that the app’s online connections are covered by this notice.
